Account & Data Deletion

How to delete your Fartmigos multiplayer account and local saves, or request help by email.

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. What account does the game create?

Fartmigos, operated by Wave Games LLC, uses an anonymous Unity multiplayer account when you play online. You do not register with an email address or password. Your game installation keeps credentials for that account, and your device stores your display name, settings, cosmetics, and other local saves.

2. Delete from inside the game

  1. Open Fartmigos on the device whose account and saves you want to delete.
  2. Leave your current game or party and return to the main menu.
  3. Open Settings and select Delete Account.
  4. Read the confirmation message and choose DELETE. Choose CANCEL if you do not want to continue.
  5. Keep an internet connection available and wait for the operation to finish. The game restarts with a fresh local state after successful deletion.

Deletion is permanent. It removes your current multiplayer account and game-owned saves on this device, including your display name, cosmetics, currency balances, and settings. If you play online again, a new anonymous account is created.

If an error appears, deletion may not have completed. Follow the message and retry. A message saying the account was deleted but local cleanup failed means you should retry to finish removing local data. If an older linked account prevents deletion, contact us rather than assuming it has been deleted.

3. What the in-game process removes

  • The current Unity Authentication account and its stored game credentials.
  • Your joined lobby memberships, or lobbies you host.
  • Game-owned saved files and preferences on the device where deletion runs, including cosmetic unlocks and selections, currency balances, display name, language, and control settings.

Deletion does not erase data from other devices, previously created backups, other players’ devices, or independently operated advertising and app-store systems. It does not delete your Apple ID or Google account.

4. Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, encounter an error, or want to delete support correspondence or other personal information we control, email contact@wavegames.io. You do not need to reinstall the game to send a request.

  • Use the subject Fartmigos Account Deletion or describe the specific data you want deleted.
  • Include your platform, device model, in-game display name, and any player identifier already available to you. Tell us if you cannot access the game.
  • Include your country so we can apply the appropriate privacy-request process.
  • Do not send passwords, session tokens, payment details, or unnecessary identity documents.

Anonymous accounts are not linked to your email by default, and a display name is not necessarily unique. We may need additional information to locate the right account and verify your request. If the available information is insufficient, we will explain what we can and cannot identify. In-app deletion is the most direct way to identify the current account while its credentials remain on your device.

We handle verified requests within the time required by applicable law and explain any applicable exception or necessary extension. An email request does not remotely wipe local files from your device.

5. Records that may remain

Deleting an authentication account does not immediately delete every record held by every provider. Unity documents retention of player IDs in Lobby logs for up to 30 days and retention of Relay personal data for 30 days. For requests relating to these service records, contact us so we can help route the request to the relevant service.

Advertising providers and app stores handle independently controlled information under their own policies and privacy controls. In-game account deletion does not send deletion requests to those providers. Links to their policies are in our Privacy Policy.

Support or other limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary to meet legal obligations, protect security, address fraud, resolve disputes, or document how a request was handled. These records are retained only for the relevant purpose and applicable retention requirements.

6. Uninstalling is different from account deletion

Uninstalling or clearing app data can remove local saves, depending on the operating system and backup settings, but does not itself request deletion of the server-side Unity account or advertising records. It may also remove the credentials needed to identify your anonymous account. Use the in-game deletion option first when possible. Restoring a device backup may restore old local saves.

7. Need help?

Contact Wave Games LLC at contact@wavegames.io or visit our contact page. For broader privacy choices, including access, correction, and advertising opt-outs, see our Privacy Policy.
