Privacy Policy

How Wave Games LLC handles information in Fartmigos, including anonymous accounts, multiplayer, advertising, and your privacy choices.

1. Who we are

Fartmigos is operated by Wave Games LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”). This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game, its online features, our related website, or contact support. For privacy questions and requests, email contact@wavegames.io.

2. Information the game uses

You choose an in-game display name. Online multiplayer uses Unity anonymous sign-in, which creates a player account without an email address, password, or registration form. “Anonymous” means that no such sign-up details are required; the account still has a persistent player identifier.

  • Account and multiplayer information: your generated player ID, authentication and reconnection credentials, room names and session identifiers, room visibility, lobby membership, selected map, game version, and gameplay state needed to connect and synchronize players. Your display name, character appearance, and gameplay actions are visible to other players in your session.
  • Local game data: your display name, settings, language, controls, cosmetic selections and unlocks, virtual currency balances, and saved session information. The current game stores these saves on your device; it does not use Unity Cloud Save for them.
  • Technical information: online and advertising providers may process IP addresses, device or installation identifiers, device model, operating system, language, app version, network details, and service diagnostics to deliver, secure, and troubleshoot their services.
  • Advertising information: ad impressions, clicks, reward events, advertising identifiers where available and permitted, approximate location inferred from IP address, consent choices, and information used for ad measurement and fraud prevention.
  • Support information: your email address and the messages, device details, screenshots, or other information you choose to send us. Please avoid including passwords, authentication tokens, payment details, or unnecessary sensitive information.

Public rooms: when a host makes a room public, its name, player count, capacity, and availability can appear in the in-game room browser. A private room uses an invitation code. Avoid putting personal or sensitive information in room names or display names.

Microphone: Fartmigos does not use your microphone or collect microphone recordings.

Our website hosting provider may process IP addresses and request information to deliver pages and protect the website. Email providers process messages sent to our support address.

3. Why information is used

  • Provide the game, remember your preferences, and operate anonymous accounts and multiplayer rooms.
  • Synchronize gameplay and help players reconnect to their sessions.
  • Deliver and measure advertisements and award the associated in-game benefits.
  • Diagnose problems, protect services, and detect cheating, fraud, or unauthorized access.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests, comply with legal obligations, and resolve disputes.

Where data protection law requires a legal basis, we rely on performance of our agreement with you for requested game services; legitimate interests for proportionate security, troubleshooting, and support; legal obligations where applicable; and consent for processing that requires it, including certain advertising and tracking activities. Agreeing to our Terms of Service does not replace a required advertising or tracking consent.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use providers to operate the game and related services. Their roles and data use depend on the service and applicable law; some process information for us, while advertising providers may also process information for their own purposes.

  • Unity Gaming Services: Authentication, multiplayer sessions, Lobby, and Relay provide anonymous sign-in, room discovery, and multiplayer connections. See Unity’s game-player privacy policy.
  • AppLovin MAX: selects and serves ads through AppLovin and mediated networks. See AppLovin’s privacy policy.
  • Mediated advertising networks: the game includes integrations for Google AdMob, Meta Audience Network, Unity Ads and ironSource, Liftoff Monetize (Vungle), and Mintegral. Which providers serve ads depends on platform, region, consent choices, availability, and the current mediation configuration.
  • Connectivity checks: the game periodically requests Google’s connectivity-check endpoint to detect whether an internet connection is available. Google receives the IP address and ordinary network-request information for that connection. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Website and support providers: Cloudflare hosts and protects this website. Our email service providers process support correspondence.

Other players receive the information needed for the multiplayer features described above. We may also disclose information when required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, our rights, and service security. If the business is reorganized or transferred, relevant information may transfer with it, subject to applicable privacy protections.

5. Advertising and your choices

The game contains interstitial and rewarded advertisements. AppLovin and its partners may use device, identifier, network, and ad-interaction data to select ads, measure results, prevent fraud, and improve advertising services, including advertising models. Their policies explain additional uses and choices. Some disclosures for personalized advertising may count as “sale,” “sharing,” or targeted advertising under applicable privacy laws.

The game integrates AppLovin’s consent flow and Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt where applicable. You can decline tracking in the system prompt or change it under iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Tracking permission is separate from other advertising consent. Declining tracking does not disable the game, and advertisements may still appear.

Use the choices presented in advertising consent screens, your device’s advertising settings, and providers’ privacy controls. AppLovin describes its opt-out options in its privacy policy. You can also email us to request withdrawal of consent or to exercise applicable rights to opt out of sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already lawfully completed.

6. How long information is kept

  • Local saves: remain until you delete them through the game’s Delete Account feature or your device’s app-data controls. Uninstallation and backup restoration behave differently across devices; restoring a backup may restore saved data.
  • Anonymous account: Unity Authentication retains the account until it is deleted. Uninstalling alone does not request server-side deletion.
  • Multiplayer records: the game leaves or removes session membership when you exit, and removes hosted lobbies during account deletion. Unity documents retention of player IDs in Lobby logs for up to 30 days and retention of Relay personal data for 30 days. These service records are separate from the account itself. See Unity’s Lobby and Relay privacy information.
  • Support and administrative records: kept for as long as reasonably needed to handle the request, maintain necessary support history, meet legal obligations, protect the service, or resolve disputes. The relevant purpose and any legal requirements determine the retention period.
  • Advertising-provider records: retained according to each provider’s purposes and policies. Deleting the game account does not automatically erase these independently held records.

7. Account and data deletion

Return to the game’s main menu, open Settings → Delete Account, read the warning, and confirm DELETE. When completed, this removes the current Unity multiplayer account, its lobby memberships or hosted lobbies, and game-owned local saves on that device. The game restarts with fresh local settings. Joining online play again creates a new anonymous account.

For requests without the app, support-message deletion, provider-held records, or problems with the in-game process, contact contact@wavegames.io. We may need information to locate the records and verify the request. See Account & Data Deletion for the full process and its limits.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a portable copy of personal information; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; and opt out of certain advertising uses. You may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority or appeal a decision on a privacy request.

Email contact@wavegames.io with “Fartmigos Privacy Request” in the subject and tell us what you are requesting. Include your platform and country so we can apply the relevant process. We may ask for proportionate verification or authorization if someone acts on your behalf. We respond within the time required by applicable law and do not penalize you for exercising a legal privacy right.

Anonymous game accounts are not linked to email addresses by default. A display name may not uniquely identify an account. If we cannot identify the relevant records, we will explain the limitation and any available next steps.

9. Children’s privacy

Younger players should involve a parent or legal guardian before using online features or sending information to support. Do not put your real name, contact details, or other sensitive information in your display name.

Children’s data is subject to additional protections under applicable law. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child’s information has been processed inappropriately, contact contact@wavegames.io. We will investigate and take appropriate steps, including deletion where required. This policy does not itself obtain parental consent or override age restrictions imposed by law or service providers.

10. Security and international processing

We use measures designed to protect information, including authenticated multiplayer sessions and encrypted service connections. No online service or storage system is completely secure. Keep your device and software up to date, and do not share authentication credentials.

Our providers operate internationally, so information may be processed outside your country. Where required by applicable law, international transfers must use appropriate safeguards. Contact us for information about safeguards relevant to your data; providers also describe their transfer arrangements in their policies.

11. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game, providers, or our practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision. We will provide additional notice or request consent where required by law.
